Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 3, 2026

Introduction

ATDW Solo Tool ("Across a Thousand Dead Worlds - Solo Play Tool") is a free companion app for the tabletop role-playing game "Across a Thousand Dead Worlds." This privacy policy explains how the app handles user data.

Data Collection

This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All data generated during use (dice rolls, tracker values, generated content) is stored locally on your device only using your browser's localStorage. No data is sent to any server.

Personal Information

We do not collect any personally identifiable information, including but not limited to: names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device identifiers.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Phone State Permission

The Android version of this app may request the READ_PHONE_STATE permission. This is used solely by the app wrapper technology to detect phone call interruptions and pause app activity accordingly. No phone data is read, stored, or transmitted.

Cookies

This app does not use cookies.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the app's repository or contact the developer.
